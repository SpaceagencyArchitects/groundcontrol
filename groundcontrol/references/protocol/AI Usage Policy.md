# AI Usage Policy

## Purpose
This policy sets out how Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools may be used within our practice to support architectural design, project management, and communications. Its goal is to maximise efficiency and innovation while ensuring client confidentiality, data security, and professional integrity.

## Scope
This policy applies to all staff, contractors, and collaborators using AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, MidJourney, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion, automated drafting assistants, transcription tools).

## Acceptable Uses of AI
AI tools may be used for:
* Design support: Concept development, precedent research, generative ideas, visualisations.
* Documentation support: Drafting text for reports, specifications, and submissions (to be reviewed and finalised by architects).
* Communication support: Drafting letters, presentations, and social media content.
* Workflow efficiency: Automating repetitive tasks such as meeting notes, file organisation, or simple data extraction.
* Learning & Research: Exploring regulations, codes, industry best practices, and new technologies (always verified against official sources).

## Prohibited Uses of AI
AI tools must not be used for:
* Making final design decisions without human oversight.
* Providing unverified technical, legal, or compliance advice.
* Replacing required professional judgment in architectural work.
* Sharing or uploading sensitive or confidential information (see below).

## Information That Must Not Be Shared with AI Tools
Unless using an approved, private, enterprise-level AI system with data protection guarantees, the following must never be shared with AI tools:

### Client & Project Information
* Client names, addresses, contact details.
* Project site addresses, title details, or geospatial identifiers.
* Financial information, including budgets, contracts, fee proposals, or commercial strategies.
* Confidential client briefs or business strategies.
* Unreleased designs, models, or proprietary drawings.

### Business & Practice Information
* Internal financial data, forecasts, or salary information.
* Legal documents, contracts, or dispute-related material.
* Tender submissions before they are public.
* Internal policies not intended for external release.

### Personal & Staff Information
* Personal contact details (emails, phone numbers, addresses).
* HR records, CVs, or private employment information.
* Health or personal circumstances of staff.

### Sensitive or Regulated Material
* Material covered by Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).
* Security-sensitive building information (e.g., prisons, embassies, government facilities).
* Information that may breach copyright, IP ownership, or client confidentiality.

## Review & Verification
* All outputs generated by AI must be reviewed by an architect or senior staff member before external use.
* AI-generated text or images must be checked for accuracy, bias, and alignment with practice values.
* Any errors, hallucinations, or misleading outputs are the responsibility of the user to identify and correct.

## Accountability
* Each team member is responsible for ensuring compliance with this policy.
* If in doubt, talk to a director.

## Policy Review
This policy will be reviewed annually, or earlier if AI technologies or data security requirements change.
