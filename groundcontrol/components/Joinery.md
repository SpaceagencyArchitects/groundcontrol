# Joinery  
  
## [[A10 - Floor and Roof Plans]] / GA Plans  
Note: There is a strong preference for identifying joinery items in A10 **1:100** general arrangement floor plans.  
* Joinery items coded, using codes listed in A60 legend  
  
## [[A60 - Internal Spaces (Room Layouts)]]
Note:   
As a second preference, in the case where coding of joinery items is not practical in A10, use A60 room layout plans to identify joinery items  
* Joinery items coded, using codes listed in A60 legend  
  
## [[A60 - Internal Spaces (Room Layouts)]] / Legend Sheet  
* Spreadsheet listing joinery codes, generic description of items and refers to drawing number in M series where the item is drawn in detail
