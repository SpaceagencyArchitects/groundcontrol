## Applicability

Applies to swimming pools, spa pools, and portable pools with water deeper than 300 mm. Barriers must restrict access to the pool/spa from the street, adjoining properties, and all habitable buildings.

## Height

- Minimum 1.2 m (non-poolside)
- 1.8 m boundary option (poolside, for full enclosure)
- Measured perpendicularly to finished ground level; no tolerance

## Gaps & openings

- Maximum 100 mm horizontal gap between vertical members
- Maximum 100 mm gap between barrier and ground
- Maximum 100 mm gap beneath gates
- Perforated material: 13 mm or less (1.2 m height) OR 13–100 mm (1.8 m height minimum)

## Gates

- Must swing outward (away from the pool)
- Must self-close when released from any open position
- Requires an automatic latching device
- Latch release positioned:
  - 1500 mm minimum above ground (non-poolside option), OR
  - Poolside with 450 mm minimum shielding radius

## Materials

- Any durable material acceptable if compliant with AS 1926.1
- Glass fences: Grade-A safety glass per AS 1288 and AS/NZS 2208, permanently fixed
- Must be permanently fixed — unable to be removed without tools

## Non-Climbable Zones (NCZ)

Apply to barriers under 1.8 m. Five zones define where climbable objects (branches, handholds, ledges, roof features) must not exist. **NCZ 2 and NCZ 3 are curved quadrant sweeps, not straight-line offsets** — check the full curve, not just the directly-overhead or directly-below case.

- **NCZ 1** — a 900 mm measurement on the barrier face itself, between horizontal components. Not a quadrant: no handholds, footholds, or protrusions on the barrier structure.
- **NCZ 2** — a 900 mm quadrant aligned with NCZ 1, sweeping away and downward from the barrier and the pool area (base of barrier, outward face).
- **NCZ 3** — a 900 mm quadrant that starts at the top of the barrier and sweeps away and upward from the barrier and the pool area, extending down to meet the top of NCZ 1/NCZ 2. Mirrors NCZ 2 at the top of the fence. **Not** simply "clear space directly above the fence" — climbable objects must be excluded both above the barrier and outward from its top edge within the swept curve (a nearby wall, planter edge, low roof, or overhanging structure within the 900 mm radius, even off to the side rather than directly overhead).
- **NCZ 4** — a 900 mm × 300 mm area on the poolside, aligning with NCZ 1. Only required where openings between vertical members exceed 10 mm.
- **NCZ 5** — boundary barriers using the poolside option only. A 900 mm lower quadrant starting at the top of the boundary fence, extending into the pool area.

Separate rule, in addition to the NCZ curves: no climbable objects within 500 mm of a non-poolside barrier face, at any height.

## Barrier placement & structural integration

- No fixed setback distance from columns, walls, or other structures is prescribed — placement is a design decision, not a regulatory minimum
- The governing test is functional: the barrier must restrict access from the street, adjoining properties, and habitable buildings
- Where a barrier meets a column or structural element:
  - Confirm secure fixing if the structure is used as an anchor point
  - Check the structure doesn't sit inside NCZ 2/NCZ 3's swept curve or the 500 mm outer clearance
  - Confirm fixings don't compromise structural integrity
  - Decide whether the barrier runs continuous or segmented around the obstruction
- Barrier must be supported on stabilised ground; check site conditions around columns or footings

## Other

- Maximum 15° lean from vertical
- No sharp edges or projections
- Stabilised ground surface beneath the barrier

## Design & documentation checklist

### Design phase
- Identify all pool/spa boundaries (street, adjoining properties, habitable buildings)
- Confirm barrier location: non-poolside (1.2 m) or boundary (1.8 m)
- Check site access points — gates required at every entry point
- Confirm material choice: solid, perforated, or glass
- For glass, specify Grade-A safety glass per AS 1288 / AS/NZS 2208
- Check for climbable features within 500 mm of the barrier and within the NCZ 2/NCZ 3 swept curves

### Detailed design & drawings
- Height dimension: 1.2 m or 1.8 m, perpendicular to finished ground
- Gap dimensions: all max 100 mm — horizontal, ground, beneath gates
- Gate detail: swings outward, self-closes, latch at 1500 mm (or poolside 450 mm shielding)
- NCZ diagram or annotation, with NCZ 2 and NCZ 3 shown as swept curves, not straight offsets
- Material spec: durability, finish, fastening (permanent, no tools to remove)
- Ground condition: stabilised surface confirmed; note site-specific requirements

### Documentation & specification
- Barrier schedule: type, material, height, gate detail
- Drawings annotated with the AS 1926 reference
- Specification note: barriers comply with AS 1926.1 / AS 1926.2 (cite current edition per `../../rules/code-currency.md`)
- Compliance verification method noted (e.g. site inspection post-construction)

### Compliance verification (process, for client advice)
- Builder's Notice of Completion submitted within 7 days of completion
- Local government inspects within 30 days
- Council conducts periodic inspections roughly every 4 years (verify current interval live)
- Owner's ongoing responsibility: latches, hinges, and gate self-closing must stay functional
