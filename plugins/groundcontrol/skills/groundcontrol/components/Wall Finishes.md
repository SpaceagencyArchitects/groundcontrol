# Wall Finishes  

  
Note: There is a strong preference for documenting wall finishes in a tabular room schedule.   
Finishes codes allocated to the rooms in the room schedule spreadsheet sufficiently describe finishes in the majority of cases.   
For more complex internal spaces (e.g. lift lobby, auditorium), instead of trying to code it, draw internal elevations at **1:50** scale in [[A60 - Internal Spaces (Room Layouts)]] and write a note in the schedule ‘FOR DETAILS REFER TO DRAWING A60.xx’  
  
### Describe Finishes  
  
## [[A00 - Introductory documents]] / Legend Sheet  
* Key - description of finishes codes; reference to specification section   
  
## [[A80 - Schedules]] / Room Schedule  
*(Covers most of the areas by allocating FINISHES codes)*  
* The wall finishes are described by listing the finishes codes under the |WALL/FINISH| criteria  
* Refer complex areas which can not be scheduled to **1:50** plans and elevations in [[A60 - Internal Spaces (Room Layouts)]] *(for more complex areas instead of trying to code it, write a note in schedule ‘FOR DETAILS REFER TO DRAWING A60.##’)*   
  
## [[A60 - Internal Spaces (Room Layouts)]] 
*(if provided)*  
* Describe areas of different wall finishes using finishes codes  

## [[Z - Specifications]]  
* Painting Specifications  
* Tiling Specifications
