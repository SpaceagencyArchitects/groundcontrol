### Door Tag  
![](Doors/5AA259CB-4D3B-4466-A0E0-91A678A0EBDB.png)  
Door type = unique identifier of door type  
Door number = room number the door is opening into + suffix.   
e.g. if room No 123 has one door opening into it, the door number will be 123.1 If room No 234 has three doors opening into it, they will be identified as 234.1, 234.2 and 234.3.  
Exceptions to the rule: e.g. accessible toilet door opening into corridor – assign door number the same as toilet room number, not corridor.  
Doors into emergency stairs should be numbered from the bottom up, eg. Doors to stair A1 will be A1.1, A1.2, A1.3 etc  
Once the door numbers are assigned, do not change them, e.g. if the door swing changes half way through the documentation, do not change the door number.  
  
  
Note:   
If door handles, thresholds and the like are shown on **1:100** plans, make sure they are switched off on reflected ceiling plans.
