# Floors   

We use 3 methods for documenting floor finishes:

Option 1
- document floor finishes in a tabular room schedule in [[A80 - Schedules]]. Often, finishes codes allocated to the rooms in the room schedule spreadsheet sufficiently describe finishes in the majority of cases.

Option 2
- For more complex internal spaces (e.g. lift lobby, auditorium), draw a plan at **1:50** scale in [[A60 - Internal Spaces (Room Layouts)]] and write a note in the schedule ‘FOR DETAILS REFER TO DRAWING A60.xx’ 

Option 3
- For areas that are not individually drawn in [[A60 - Internal Spaces (Room Layouts)]], another ‘Floor Finishes’ drawing series. Depending on the scale of these plans, they can inserted as a subset of the A1 or A6 series. Floor finishes drawings can include skirtings and other wall protection elements.

## [[A00 - Introductory documents]] / Legend Sheet  
* Key - description of flooring codes; reference to specification section   
* Key - description of the construction/substrate code the finish is applied to - e.g. code for cement screed, raised floor, etc   
  
## [[A60 - Internal Spaces (Room Layouts)]]
*(if provided - otherwise refer direct from **1:100** plans in A10 - Floor and Roof Plans)*   
* Coded and graphic representation of various flooring systems  
* Discrete codes for substrates and for finishes  
  
Note:   
In the case where the majority of the floor finishes are too complex to schedule, as a second preference we resort to documenting them through graphic representation. This approach creates another subset of **1:100** plans in **A60**, used exclusively to identify floor finishes.  
  
## [[A80 - Schedules]] 
* Insert codes in room schedule: |FLOOR/SUBSTRATE| and  |FLOOR/FINISH|  
  
## [[A50 - Interface Details]]
* Floor-to-external wall composite section details **1:5** *(construction of the detail only - do not describe applied finishes e.g. paint, sealant, etc.)*  
* Flooring (floor-to-floor) details **1:5** *(construction of the detail only - do not describe applied finishes e.g. paint, sealant, etc.)*  
* Partitions-to-floors interface composite details **1:5** *(construction of the detail only - do not describe applied finishes e.g. paint, sealant, etc.)*
