## FURNISHED PLANS (1:100)  
  
Required only if not possible to show and code FFE items in [[A10 - Floor and Roof Plans]] or [[A60 - Internal Spaces (Room Layouts)]].

- Furniture and fixtures, fittings and equipment (FFE) codes
